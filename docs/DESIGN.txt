════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                              VISUAL PRINCIPLES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

From your brief:

1. EVERY ELEMENT EARNS ITS PLACE
   Nothing decorative. If it's on the page, it's doing work.

2. HIERARCHY IS MEANING
   Most important = biggest, top. Squint test.

3. TYPOGRAPHY IS THE INTERFACE
   Words do the work. Minimal icons.

4. INFORMATION DENSITY WITH CALM
   Show a lot without overwhelm. Intentional, not cluttered.

5. NO DECORATION, ONLY DECISIONS
   No gradients, shadows, rounded corners unless they mean something.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                              COLOR USAGE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YES / Positive:         Green or accent color
MAYBE / Neutral:        Yellow or muted
NO / Negative:          Gray (not red — it's not an error)
ATTENDING:              Solid badge, confident

Judgment badges:
    [YES]   ← green/teal background, white text
    [MAYBE] ← yellow/amber background, dark text
    [NO]    ← gray background, muted text (if shown at all)
